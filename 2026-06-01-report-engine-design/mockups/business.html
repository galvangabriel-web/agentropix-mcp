OrientationHow to read this report

This tier scores each finding on two independent axes that ADR-024 mandates be kept separate. Conflating them is a reporting error.

Likelihood (FIRST 5-tier) — estimative probability the risk event occurs / recurs if unremediated: almost_certain > highly_likely > likely > unlikely > remote

Analytic confidence (LCA) — trust in the evidence & reasoning, independent of likelihood: High · Moderate · Low

A finding can be highly likely with only Moderate confidence. Inherent risk = Likelihood × Impact before controls. Residual risk = what remains after the recommended remediation. Relative cost = Low / Med / High effort + disruption.

Section 1Executive risk posture

The SRL-2018 intrusion is a fully realized domain compromise with ~143 days of undetected dwell. An external actor entered through an internet-facing DMZ FTP host, established credential-theft and scheduled-task persistence on the domain controller, planted six backdoor local accounts, and stood up a Cobalt-Strike-class C2 beacon. Domain trust integrity can no longer be assumed — every Kerberos ticket, cached credential, and trust relationship in shieldbase.lan is potentially attacker-controlled until the krbtgt secret is rotated.

Section 6 · enrichmentStrategic recommendations

  1. Treat the domain as compromised, not the host. Sequence around krbtgt rotation and estate-wide credential reset (R1/R5), not single-server cleanup.
  2. Close the 143-day detection gap as a program, not a patch (R7). Cmdline auditing + tamper-evident off-host logging + beacon-cadence analytics would have surfaced a 36-second jitter-free beacon in hours.
  3. Retire the flat DMZ→internal trust (R6). Highest-leverage architectural change: converts "one FTP foothold = domain compromise" into "one FTP foothold = one contained host."
  4. Stand up the breach-notification track on day one (R4). The regulatory clock, not technical eradication, is the dominant business-liability driver.
  5. Adopt this tiered report cadence. Pair with Executive and Analyst tiers so leadership decisions and technical evidence stay provably linked (ADR-024).
