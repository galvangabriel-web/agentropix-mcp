LEGENDLikelihood & Confidence

Likelihood scale (FIRST 5-tier): almost_certain > highly_likely > likely > unlikely > remote. Likelihood estimates the probability of the assessed activity; it is kept separate from analytic confidence.

Confidence (LCA): high / moderate / low — the analyst's confidence in the assessment given evidence quality and corroboration. Distinct from likelihood.
These are the exact two blockquote axes the emitter auto-inserts (LIKELIHOOD_LEGEND + CONFIDENCE_LEGEND, markdown.py). They are never collapsed into one number: a beacon can be highly_likely C2 yet held at moderate confidence because the C2 endpoint was unreachable for active confirmation (see F-03). The Finding view model enforces both as closed Literals, so a typo cannot invent a sixth band.
