01 · Executive Dashboard

Executive Summary

Single-host triage of a 511 MiB raw Windows XP-era physical memory image (sample001.bin, the Art-of-Memory-Forensics teaching sample). Scope is RAM only — there is no on-disk corpus and no live host. Agentropix recovered 21 running processes, 229 services (118 running), a coherent two-root PPID forest (0 orphans, 0 LOLBin flags) and all 21 command lines, then ran malfind over the image and surfaced 15 executable VAD regions — 11 PAGE_EXECUTE_READWRITE (RWX) and 4 PAGE_EXECUTE_READ (RX) — concentrated in winlogon.exe (PID 628). Six findings were promoted and rolled up into this sealed full-profile report. No network endpoints and no IOCs were recoverable from this image.

Headline: 9 RWX (PAGE_EXECUTE_READWRITE) injected/unpacked-code regions in winlogon.exe (PID 628) — the dominant, canonical process-injection signal (MITRE T1055) in this image — alongside single RWX regions in csrss.exe and msimn.exe. Severity is capped at medium: this is a known teaching sample and the highest-confidence signal is memory-resident with no corroborating network or on-disk artefact.
